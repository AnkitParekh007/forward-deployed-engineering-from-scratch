# Community code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive and inclusive. Welcome beginners and people from different backgrounds. Critique ideas with evidence rather than attacking people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, publishing personal information without consent, and spam are unacceptable. Report concerns privately to the repository maintainer using a suitable private contact method. Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation proportionately; protect reporter privacy and avoid retaliation. This applies to repository discussions, issues and community events.
