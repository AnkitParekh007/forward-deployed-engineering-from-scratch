# Capstone evaluation and rollout

## 💡 Why this matters
A customer asks whether the pilot is ready.

## 🎯 Learning objectives
- Explain capstone evaluation and rollout in your own words.
- Apply the concept to the customer scenario.
- Produce and independently verify the requested evidence.

## 🧰 Prerequisites
Complete lesson 12-03: Capstone implementation; use synthetic data.

## 🧠 Concepts
Measure representative outcomes, record limitations and prepare deployment, monitoring and rollback. Separate simulated from real outcomes.

## 🛠️ Build it
1. Read the scenario and identify the input and expected output.
2. Run ten fixtures, calculate success rate and record a go/no-go decision.
3. Verify the result using the success evidence below.

## 🌐 Real-world scenario
A customer asks whether the pilot is ready. An FDE documents assumptions, tests the smallest useful change and reviews the outcome with the workflow owner.

## ⚠️ Common mistakes
- Treating a generated result as verified evidence. Check it independently.
- Using real customer data in a learning demo. Use synthetic records.
- Hiding missing evidence. Document limitations and ask for review.

## 🧪 Checkpoint
1. What is the denominator of success rate?
2. What evidence would show your exercise works?
3. What should you do if the evidence fails?

## 📦 Deliverable
Evaluation table, deployment checklist and rollback plan.
