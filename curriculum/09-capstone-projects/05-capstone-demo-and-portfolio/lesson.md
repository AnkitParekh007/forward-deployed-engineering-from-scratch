# Capstone demo and portfolio

## Why this matters
An interviewer wants proof of FDE thinking.

## Learning objectives
- Explain capstone demo and portfolio in your own words.
- Apply the concept to the customer scenario.
- Produce and independently verify the requested evidence.

## Prerequisites
Complete lesson 12-04: Capstone evaluation and rollout; use synthetic data.

## Concepts
Explain customer need, architecture, results, limitation and next iteration. Label synthetic evidence honestly.

## Build it
1. Read the scenario and identify the input and expected output.
2. Publish your walkthrough, tests, architecture and retrospective with a truthful resume bullet.
3. Verify the result using the success evidence below.

## Real-world scenario
An interviewer wants proof of FDE thinking. An FDE documents assumptions, tests the smallest useful change and reviews the outcome with the workflow owner.

## Common mistakes
- Treating a generated result as verified evidence. Check it independently.
- Using real customer data in a learning demo. Use synthetic records.
- Hiding missing evidence. Document limitations and ask for review.

## Checkpoint
1. Why distinguish simulated from real outcomes?
2. What evidence would show your exercise works?
3. What should you do if the evidence fails?

## Deliverable
A portfolio link and three improvement ideas.
