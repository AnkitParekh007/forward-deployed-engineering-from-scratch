## 🛠️ Mini-project
Combine the five lesson deliverables into a Customer issue triage evidence bundle.

## 🌐 Real FDE scenario
A retailer asks for an AI chatbot but its actual bottleneck is stock lookup.

## ✅ Success criteria
- A named user and measurable outcome, not a preferred technology.
- A metric with units and a target.
- A clear in-scope and out-of-scope list.
- Three recorded input-output checks.
- A one-page brief with a decision request.
