# Your first field brief

## Why this matters
The support manager wants an immediate improvement.

## Learning objectives
- Explain your first field brief in your own words.
- Apply the concept to the customer scenario.
- Produce and independently verify the requested evidence.

## Prerequisites
Complete lesson 01-04: AI-assisted learning; use synthetic data.

## Concepts
A field brief records a problem, constraints, owner, test and decision. It makes ambiguity visible.

## Build it
1. Read the scenario and identify the input and expected output.
2. Produce a field brief for ticket triage using synthetic tickets.
3. Verify the result using the success evidence below.

## Real-world scenario
The support manager wants an immediate improvement. An FDE documents assumptions, tests the smallest useful change and reviews the outcome with the workflow owner.

## Common mistakes
- Treating a generated result as verified evidence. Check it independently.
- Using real customer data in a learning demo. Use synthetic records.
- Hiding missing evidence. Document limitations and ask for review.

## Checkpoint
1. What should a field brief end with?
2. What evidence would show your exercise works?
3. What should you do if the evidence fails?

## Deliverable
A one-page brief with a decision request.
