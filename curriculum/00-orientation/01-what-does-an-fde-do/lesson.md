# What does an FDE do?

## Why this matters
A retailer asks for an AI chatbot but its actual bottleneck is stock lookup.

## Learning objectives
- Explain what does an fde do? in your own words.
- Apply the concept to the customer scenario.
- Produce and independently verify the requested evidence.

## Prerequisites
No prior coding experience required.

## Concepts
An FDE owns the boundary between customer needs and delivered software. Discovery, implementation, integration and feedback are one continuous loop.

## Build it
1. Read the scenario and identify the input and expected output.
2. Write a one-page workflow map: user, trigger, current steps, pain and desired outcome.
3. Verify the result using the success evidence below.

## Real-world scenario
A retailer asks for an AI chatbot but its actual bottleneck is stock lookup. An FDE documents assumptions, tests the smallest useful change and reviews the outcome with the workflow owner.

## Common mistakes
- Treating a generated result as verified evidence. Check it independently.
- Using real customer data in a learning demo. Use synthetic records.
- Hiding missing evidence. Document limitations and ask for review.

## Checkpoint
1. What distinguishes an FDE from a model researcher?
2. What evidence would show your exercise works?
3. What should you do if the evidence fails?

## Deliverable
A named user and measurable outcome, not a preferred technology.
