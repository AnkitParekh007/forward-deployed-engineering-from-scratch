# Map a business workflow

## 💡 Why this matters
A supplier submits incomplete documents.

## 🎯 Learning objectives
- Explain map a business workflow in your own words.
- Apply the concept to the customer scenario.
- Produce and independently verify the requested evidence.

## 🧰 Prerequisites
Complete lesson 04-02: Requirements under ambiguity; use synthetic data.

## 🧠 Concepts
A workflow has actors, events, decisions and handoffs. Diagram the current system before proposing changes.

## 🛠️ Build it
1. Read the scenario and identify the input and expected output.
2. Map the current process and mark one manual review step.
3. Verify the result using the success evidence below.

## 🌐 Real-world scenario
A supplier submits incomplete documents. An FDE documents assumptions, tests the smallest useful change and reviews the outcome with the workflow owner.

## ⚠️ Common mistakes
- Treating a generated result as verified evidence. Check it independently.
- Using real customer data in a learning demo. Use synthetic records.
- Hiding missing evidence. Document limitations and ask for review.

## 🧪 Checkpoint
1. Why map existing exceptions?
2. What evidence would show your exercise works?
3. What should you do if the evidence fails?

## 📦 Deliverable
A current-state diagram.
