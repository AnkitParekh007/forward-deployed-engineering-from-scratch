# Discovery interviews

## 💡 Why this matters
A logistics manager says shipments are slow.

## 🎯 Learning objectives
- Explain discovery interviews in your own words.
- Apply the concept to the customer scenario.
- Produce and independently verify the requested evidence.

## 🧰 Prerequisites
Complete lesson 03-05: Continuous integration; use synthetic data.

## 🧠 Concepts
Ask about observed recent work, not desired features. Capture users, exceptions and manual handoffs.

## 🛠️ Build it
1. Read the scenario and identify the input and expected output.
2. Simulate an interview with five open questions and record observed facts separately from assumptions.
3. Verify the result using the success evidence below.

## 🌐 Real-world scenario
A logistics manager says shipments are slow. An FDE documents assumptions, tests the smallest useful change and reviews the outcome with the workflow owner.

## ⚠️ Common mistakes
- Treating a generated result as verified evidence. Check it independently.
- Using real customer data in a learning demo. Use synthetic records.
- Hiding missing evidence. Document limitations and ask for review.

## 🧪 Checkpoint
1. Why ask for a recent example?
2. What evidence would show your exercise works?
3. What should you do if the evidence fails?

## 📦 Deliverable
A workflow discovery note.
