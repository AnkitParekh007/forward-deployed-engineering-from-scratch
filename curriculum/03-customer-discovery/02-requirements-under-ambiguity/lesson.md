# Requirements under ambiguity

## Why this matters
A customer says make onboarding faster.

## Learning objectives
- Explain requirements under ambiguity in your own words.
- Apply the concept to the customer scenario.
- Produce and independently verify the requested evidence.

## Prerequisites
Complete lesson 04-01: Discovery interviews; use synthetic data.

## Concepts
A requirement is testable when it states a condition and expected behavior. Unknowns need owners.

## Build it
1. Read the scenario and identify the input and expected output.
2. Rewrite the request into three acceptance criteria and two unresolved questions.
3. Verify the result using the success evidence below.

## Real-world scenario
A customer says make onboarding faster. An FDE documents assumptions, tests the smallest useful change and reviews the outcome with the workflow owner.

## Common mistakes
- Treating a generated result as verified evidence. Check it independently.
- Using real customer data in a learning demo. Use synthetic records.
- Hiding missing evidence. Document limitations and ask for review.

## Checkpoint
1. What makes a requirement testable?
2. What evidence would show your exercise works?
3. What should you do if the evidence fails?

## Deliverable
Criteria with measurable pass/fail conditions.
