# Data privacy and minimization

## 💡 Why this matters
A support export includes personal contact details.

## 🎯 Learning objectives
- Explain data privacy and minimization in your own words.
- Apply the concept to the customer scenario.
- Produce and independently verify the requested evidence.

## 🧰 Prerequisites
Complete lesson 09-05: Incident response; use synthetic data.

## 🧠 Concepts
Collect only fields needed for the task, define retention and avoid sensitive data in logs and external prompts.

## 🛠️ Build it
1. Read the scenario and identify the input and expected output.
2. Redact unnecessary synthetic fields before logging or model use.
3. Verify the result using the success evidence below.

## 🌐 Real-world scenario
A support export includes personal contact details. An FDE documents assumptions, tests the smallest useful change and reviews the outcome with the workflow owner.

## ⚠️ Common mistakes
- Treating a generated result as verified evidence. Check it independently.
- Using real customer data in a learning demo. Use synthetic records.
- Hiding missing evidence. Document limitations and ask for review.

## 🧪 Checkpoint
1. Why minimize data?
2. What evidence would show your exercise works?
3. What should you do if the evidence fails?

## 📦 Deliverable
A before/after data inventory.
