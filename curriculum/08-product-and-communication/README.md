## Real FDE scenario

## Success criteria
- Two audience-specific messages.
- A script and demo evidence.
- A feedback-to-change decision record.
- A transparent cost model with units.
- A checklist another learner can follow.

## Phase quiz
- Why state uncertainty?
- Why show a failure path?
- Why confirm feedback with evidence?
- Is estimated savings the same as realized ROI?
- Why include rollback steps?
