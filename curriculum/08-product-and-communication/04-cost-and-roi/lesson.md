# Cost and ROI

## 💡 Why this matters
A team hopes to save five minutes per ticket.

## 🎯 Learning objectives
- Explain cost and roi in your own words.
- Apply the concept to the customer scenario.
- Produce and independently verify the requested evidence.

## 🧰 Prerequisites
Complete lesson 11-03: Iteration and feedback; use synthetic data.

## 🧠 Concepts
Estimate total cost including model calls, review, infrastructure and maintenance. Compare against measured benefit, with explicit assumptions.

## 🛠️ Build it
1. Read the scenario and identify the input and expected output.
2. Calculate monthly savings for 1000 tickets and include manual review costs.
3. Verify the result using the success evidence below.

## 🌐 Real-world scenario
A team hopes to save five minutes per ticket. An FDE documents assumptions, tests the smallest useful change and reviews the outcome with the workflow owner.

## ⚠️ Common mistakes
- Treating a generated result as verified evidence. Check it independently.
- Using real customer data in a learning demo. Use synthetic records.
- Hiding missing evidence. Document limitations and ask for review.

## 🧪 Checkpoint
1. Is estimated savings the same as realized ROI?
2. What evidence would show your exercise works?
3. What should you do if the evidence fails?

## 📦 Deliverable
A transparent cost model with units.
