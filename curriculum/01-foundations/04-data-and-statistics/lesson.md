# Data and statistics

## Why this matters
One ticket waits 90 minutes while others take 3–5 minutes.

## Learning objectives
- Explain data and statistics in your own words.
- Apply the concept to the customer scenario.
- Produce and independently verify the requested evidence.

## Prerequisites
Complete lesson 02-03: TypeScript essentials; use synthetic data.

## Concepts
Mean is sensitive to outliers; median is robust. A sample is evidence, not a guarantee about a population.

## Build it
1. Read the scenario and identify the input and expected output.
2. Calculate mean and median of 3,4,5,5,90 and explain the difference.
3. Verify the result using the success evidence below.

## Real-world scenario
One ticket waits 90 minutes while others take 3–5 minutes. An FDE documents assumptions, tests the smallest useful change and reviews the outcome with the workflow owner.

## Common mistakes
- Treating a generated result as verified evidence. Check it independently.
- Using real customer data in a learning demo. Use synthetic records.
- Hiding missing evidence. Document limitations and ask for review.

## Checkpoint
1. Which statistic reflects a typical wait here?
2. What evidence would show your exercise works?
3. What should you do if the evidence fails?

## Deliverable
Mean 21.4 and median 5.
